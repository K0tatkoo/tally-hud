# Changelog

Every release of Tally HUD, newest first. Versions are MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH: MAJOR when something
people rely on changes or goes away, MINOR for something new, PATCH for a fix.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-10

First public release, for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric).

- Head-up display for elytra flight: conformal horizon and pitch ladder, flight path marker,
  heading, speed and altitude tapes, bank scale, G and angle of attack, rockets and elytra time.
- Camera banking into turns.
- Radar in a cone ahead of the nose, line of sight only, with a scope; lock on with Left Alt,
  lock and drop tones, an off-screen pointer to the locked target, and an aim point (a firing
  solution with a bow, crossbow or trident). Nothing moves your view.
- Warnings: terrain and pull-up, low altitude, stall horn, a nearby player you can see, being aimed
  at (SPIKE), elytra wear, rockets low. Voice callouts by Piper TTS with the Kristin voice
  (trained on public-domain LibriVox recordings).
